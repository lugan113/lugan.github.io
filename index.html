What We Do

GPT-4V scores 22% on industrial defect detection. The factory needs 98%+. Generic models hit a structural ceiling in specialized domains. The people who could train expert models — QC engineers, radiologists, agronomists — are locked out by the code barrier. KeenForge replaces the entire ML pipeline with one AI agent.
